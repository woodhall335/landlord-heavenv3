Before the claim is filed
1. Confirm Rebecca Mumford still occupies the Property and that no new disability, vulnerability,
safeguarding, disrepair, harassment or counterclaim issue has arisen.
2. Complete the N215 from what actually happened on 22 June 2026. Do not guess the method of service.
Attach any contemporaneous proof such as a posting certificate, delivery record, photograph, email or
statement from the server.
3. Check that the copy of the notice placed before the court is the notice actually served, including both
continuation sheets. The unchanged digital original is included separately.
4. Check compliance with every legal requirement that applied when proceedings are issued. The tenancy
agreement says no deposit was taken, so deposit protection is not relied upon. Confirm any other duties
referred to in Form 3A have been met.
5. Sign the N5 and N119 only after the N215 and evidence are complete. Pay the current county-court
possession fee of GBP 415 or provide a valid Help with Fees reference. Recheck the fee immediately before
filing.
6. File at Bristol Civil and Family Justice Centre, 2 Redcliff Street, Bristol, BS1 6GR, or use the filing method
confirmed by the court. The civil possessions enquiry email is BristolPossessions@justice.gov.uk. Ask the
court how it wants payment and how many paper copies if filing by post or at the counter.

7. The Form 3A states that proceedings must be started within 12 months of service. Do not leave filing until
the final day. On the stated service date, the practical deadline is before 22 June 2027, subject to any lawful
extension such as a relevant breathing-space period.
Evidence limitations
The management, contractor and resident materials are useful contemporaneous records but the makers are
not presently giving signed witness evidence. The hearsay notice addresses that procedural position; it does
not remove the court's ability to give the material less weight. The strongest improvement would still be a
signed statement or original unredacted email from any maker, but the claim can be presented honestly
without pretending that evidence exists.
The court decides whether Ground 14 is proved and whether possession is reasonable. This pack prepares
the documents; it does not guarantee an order or replace representation at a contested hearing.
