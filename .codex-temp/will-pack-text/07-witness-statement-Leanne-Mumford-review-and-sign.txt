Witness statement of Leanne Mumford
In Bristol County Court and Family Court | Claim number: ____________________ | William Mumford and Leanne
Mumford v Rebecca Mumford
Introduction
1. I am Leanne Mumford, one of the joint Claimants and joint landlords of the Property. I make this statement
from matters within my own knowledge except where I identify information supplied by another person or
contained in a document. Where I refer to those documents, I do not claim personal knowledge of every
underlying observation.
Tenancy and notice
2. The Defendant is Rebecca Mumford. She entered into a written tenancy agreement for the Property dated
1 April 2024 at a rent of GBP 850 per calendar month. The agreement records that no deposit was taken.
The fixed term ended on 31 March 2025 and the tenancy continued periodically.
3. A Form 3A notice dated 22 June 2026 was served on the Defendant with continuation sheets. The notice
referred to Grounds 13 and 14. The present claim relies on Ground 14 only. The unchanged notice is at
pages 4 to 15 of the Updated Evidence Bundle. The service method must be proved by the completed N215
and any contemporaneous supporting record.
Communications received and retained
4. A management email addressed to Mr and Mrs W Mumford and referring to Machin Mews Flat 7 is at page
20 of the bundle. It records reports from residents and building staff concerning cat fouling in common areas,
a health and sanitation concern, and aggressive behaviour towards cleaning staff. It requested immediate
steps.
5. An email supplied to me concerning an electrician's attempted communal remedial work is at page 22. It
refers to a visit on 23 March 2026 and a second visit, says the smell had worsened and says communal
remedial work could not be completed until the area was cleaned.
6. A BNS maintenance portal record dated 23 March 2026 is at page 24. It schedules a return visit for 10
April 2026 and refers to a smell from Flat 7 and the need to clean the area before testing could be completed.
Neighbour complaint and condition material
7. The Flat 8 complaint screenshot is at page 26. It was provided to us as a complaint concerning Flat 7. The
screenshot reports repeated strong odour of cat faeces, faeces in the communal hallway, flies, smell entering
Flat 8 and an adverse effect on the person living there and visits from his children. The screenshot supplied
to us does not display the author's identity or date.
8. An undated property-condition photograph retained in our records is at page 28. I rely on it only as limited
background because its date and maker are not established by the image itself.
Hearsay position
9. We have not obtained a signed statement from BNS, the electrician, a cleaner or the Flat 8 complainant.
The documents are therefore relied upon as hearsay and that limitation is expressly disclosed. A notice
identifying the evidence and why the makers are not presently being called has been prepared. If any maker
later provides a signed statement or agrees to attend, we will disclose and serve it in accordance with the
court's directions.

Why possession is sought
10. I ask the Court to consider the combined effect of the records. They concern hygiene in shared residential
space, the impact on neighbouring occupiers and visitors, reported conduct towards cleaning staff, and the
reported prevention of communal electrical remedial work. The management warning shows that steps were
requested. The material indicates a repeated rather than isolated problem.
11. I believe that the reported conduct has caused, or was likely to cause, nuisance or annoyance within
Ground 14. I ask the Court to make a possession order and award the court fee and such costs as it
considers appropriate.
Statement of truth
I believe that the facts stated in this witness statement are true. I understand that proceedings for contempt of
court may be brought against anyone who makes, or causes to be made, a false statement in a document
verified by a statement of truth without an honest belief in its truth.
