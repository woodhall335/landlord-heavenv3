CPR 33.2 and CPR 33.6 | William Mumford and Leanne Mumford v Rebecca Mumford | Claim number:
____________________
Notice
The Claimants give notice under CPR 33.2(3) that they intend to rely on the hearsay evidence identified
below. They also give notice under CPR 33.6 of their intention to rely on the identified photograph. This
notice is to be served no later than the date directed for service of witness statements, or earlier if the Court
directs.
Managing agent warning email
A screenshot of a management email addressed to Mr and Mrs W Mumford concerning Flat 7. It records
reports from residents and building staff of cat fouling in common areas, health and sanitation concerns and
aggressive behaviour towards cleaning staff. The maker is not presently being called because the Claimants
do not have a signed statement from the management company and no representative has agreed to attend.
Electrician and remedial-work email
A screenshot of an email supplied to Leanne Mumford referring to attempted communal remedial work on 23
March 2026 and a second visit. It states that work could not be completed until the area was cleaned. The
maker and underlying electrician are not presently being called because the Claimants have not obtained
signed statements from them.
BNS maintenance portal record
A screenshot of a BNS maintenance-system record dated 23 March 2026, scheduling attendance on 10 April
2026 and referring to smell from Flat 7 and cleaning required before testing. The author is not presently being
called because the Claimants have not obtained a signed statement from the author or a BNS records
witness.
Flat 8 complaint
A supplied screenshot reporting the experience of the owner of Flat 8 and a family member residing there.
The screenshot reports persistent cat-faeces odour, faeces in the communal hallway, flies and smell entering
Flat 8. The screenshot supplied does not display the author's identity or date. The maker is not presently
being called because the Claimants do not have the unredacted original or a signed statement and cannot
identify the maker from the retained copy.
Property photograph
The Claimants intend to rely on an undated photograph showing the condition of an internal window or bay
area at the Property only as limited background material. The date and maker are not established by the
image itself. It is not relied upon as an independent basis for Ground 13 in this claim.
Availability and copies
Copies of all identified materials are included in the Updated Evidence Bundle dated 9 November 2026. If a
maker later agrees to give evidence, or the Court directs that a maker be called, the Claimants will comply
with the Court's directions. The Claimants understand that the absence of a maker may affect the weight
given to the evidence.
