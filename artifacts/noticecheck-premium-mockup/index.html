Questions

Before you upload.

Straight answers about the free review, your documents and what the service can and cannot do.

Is the initial review really free?

Yes. You can receive the initial notice review without entering card details.

Does this replace a solicitor?

No. The checker provides legal information and document-support tools, not representation.

What documents can I upload?

PDFs and clear JPG or PNG photographs of every page you received.

How quickly will I get my review?

Most readable notices can be processed within minutes.

What happens to my documents?

Documents are encrypted and subject to clear automatic-deletion controls.

What if my hearing is urgent?

The service will direct urgent cases to appropriate housing and legal-support organisations.
