Paragraphs 4.1 and 8.1 - appendix reference
The Claimants rely on the attached Formal Appendix Bundle, indexed as Appendices A to I. Appendix A is a copy
of the Form 3A notice seeking possession already served on 22 June 2026; it is not a newly served notice. The
current August 2026 material is contained in Appendices G and H and is identified as supplied source material
pending original records and signed witness evidence.
Service evidence
Appendix B explains that completed service evidence must be provided before issue. A blank N215 is not evidence
of service and is not filed in the formal appendix bundle.
Grounds relied on
The Claimants rely on Grounds 13 and 14 in Schedule 2 to the Housing Act 1988, with Ground 14 as the principal
ground. The Court is asked to consider the evidence in the Appendix Bundle together with the N119 and any
witness evidence filed before the hearing.
This continuation sheet is to be filed with the N119 and the indexed appendix bundle.
